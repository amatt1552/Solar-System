Keveer

With an ocean encompassing the entire planet living can be complicated for most land dwellers. The cold areas are unforgiving, and the vents scalding.

Properties:

Biomes: Oceanic, Ice Shelves, Hydrothermal Trenches

Average Temperature: -10 to 40°C (warmer around vents)

Gravity: 1.02g

Day Length: 39 hours

Composition: Aquatic Terran

Atmosphere: safe

  • 74% Nitrogen
  • 18% Oxygen
  • 5% Hydrogen
  • 3% Ammonia vapor
  • Trace Methane and Salt Crystals (corrosive to electronics)

Known Humanoid Presences:

Scattered underwater Frikke villages and Dhozan deep-sea excavation crews ​

Cities:

  • MONOI – The largest undersea city on Keveer, populated almost entirely by Frikke. Population ~1.2 Million
  • QUELLE – Tourist city located on a large island in the planet-wide ocean. Around 300,000 population mostly inhabited by Frikke, Humans, Dhozan, and Korran

Moons:

  • JUSANT – Icy moon responsible for Keveer's aggressive tide swells.
